Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains how information is used when you contact Aurum, request an event, submit feedback, or use our staff portal.

Information you provide

Our forms collect the details you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number, message, event preferences, and feedback. Please share only information needed for your request and avoid including sensitive personal details in messages.

How information is used

Information submitted through the website is used to respond to inquiries, verify email addresses, discuss and manage event requests, and review feedback. Staff account information is used to authenticate users and manage access to the staff portal.

Verification and browser storage

Some requests require a verification code sent to your email address. Staff sign-in uses browser local storage to retain authentication tokens. Use the sign-out option when leaving a shared device; clearing browser storage also removes locally stored sign-in information.

Access and service providers

Handling your request may require access by the Aurum team and providers supporting website hosting, email delivery, and event coordination. If your event requires sharing guest or supplier details, discuss those requirements with your event manager before providing them.

Retention and privacy requests

Information may be needed while an inquiry, event, or account remains active and for related business records. To ask about information held about you, request a correction or deletion, or discuss retention, use our contact form. We may need to verify your identity before handling a request.

External links and updates

Links to external services, including maps, take you to services with their own privacy policies. This policy may be updated as our website and services change. The date above identifies the latest version.
